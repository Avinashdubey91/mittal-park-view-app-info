CommunityHub

CommunityHub is a private billing and record-keeping app used internally by Mittal Park View Residency, Jamshedpur, to track flat-wise maintenance bills and payments. It is not a public product and is not distributed to anyone outside the Society's own office.

Privacy Policy

The app keeps its own encrypted backup of the Society's billing records in a private Google Drive folder. That folder is shared only with the two Google accounts used by the Society's own collectors, and with no one else.

No data from this app is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics. The app does not track its users.

Questions about this app or its data can be sent to avipgdac091@gmail.com.
